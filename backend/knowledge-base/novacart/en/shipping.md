# Shipping and delivery

NovaCart prepares in-stock orders within two business days. Standard delivery normally takes three to seven business days after dispatch. Tracking becomes available after the carrier scans the parcel.

## Address changes

A shipping address may be changed only while every item is unfulfilled. Shipped, partially fulfilled, cancelled, delivered, or refunded orders cannot have their address changed. Contact support if a parcel is delayed; a weather delay can move the estimated delivery date.
