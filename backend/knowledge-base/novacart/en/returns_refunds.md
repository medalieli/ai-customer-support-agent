# Returns and refund requests

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery for eligible items in resalable condition. Original shipping charges are not refundable. A request records the customer's intent and does not guarantee approval or move money.

## Exceptions

Final-sale products cannot be returned. Orders already refunded cannot receive another refund request. Damaged products follow the damaged-item procedure. Support manually reviews partial fulfillment, missing items, and uncertain delivery dates.
